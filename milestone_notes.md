# Milestone 1 — Community Observation

## Community

r/nba

I chose r/nba because it is a large public basketball community with a wide variety of discussion. I read around 40 posts and comments before deciding on any labels.

## Candidate distinctions I observed

1. **Evidence-backed reasoning vs. unsupported claims**

   Some posts support an opinion with statistics, player performance, game events, matchups, injuries, or other specific basketball information. Other posts make a strong claim without explaining why.

2. **Detailed discussion vs. immediate reaction**

   Some posts explain an idea or opinion in multiple sentences and give reasoning. Others are short reactions expressing excitement, frustration, surprise, humor, or disappointment.

3. **Analysis vs. prediction**

   Some posts discuss why something happened in a completed game or how a player or team performed. Others focus on what they think will happen in the future, such as playoff results, awards, trades, or player performance.

4. **Specific/checkable information vs. subjective opinion**

   Some posts include statistics, scores, records, dates, or specific game events that could be checked. Others mainly express personal judgment or preference.
